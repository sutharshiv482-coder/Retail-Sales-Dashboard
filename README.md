
# 🛒 Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard** built with **SQL, Power BI, and DAX** to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer value, product categories, and regional trends.

The project transforms retail transaction data into **business-focused KPIs and actionable insights** to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Retail businesses need to continuously monitor sales, profitability, customer performance, and regional demand to identify growth opportunities and potential performance issues.

This project analyzes retail sales data to answer key business questions such as:

- Which product categories generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which states contribute the most to overall sales?
- Which customers generate the highest revenue?
- How does profitability change over time?
- Which months show comparatively lower profit margins?
- Where should the business focus marketing, customer retention, and operational efforts?

The final output is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that provides stakeholders with a consolidated view of business performance.

# 📈 Key Business Insights

### 📦 Product Performance

* **Electronics** is the strongest-performing product category, contributing **38.58% of total sales** and **38.86% of total profit**.
* Its strong contribution to both revenue and profit indicates that Electronics is a major driver of business performance and should remain a priority for **inventory planning, product expansion, and future investment**.

### 🌍 Regional Performance

* **Wisconsin** generated the highest share of sales at **20.74%**, followed by **Missouri (20.11%)** and **Georgia (20.00%)**.
* These states represent the strongest sales markets and should receive greater attention through **targeted marketing, customer retention, and inventory planning**.

### 👥 Customer Analysis

* A relatively small group of high-value customers contributes significantly to overall revenue.
* Analyzing the **Top 5 Customers** can reveal purchasing patterns and help the business develop **personalized offers and loyalty strategies**.
* Retaining high-value customers can help protect recurring revenue and increase long-term customer value.

### 📈 Profitability Trend

* Profit margins varied across different months and years, indicating differences in monthly business performance.

* The lowest-profit months identified were:

  * **May 2021 — 19.09%**
  * **February 2022 — 19.43%**
  * **January 2023 — 19.89%**
  * **April 2024 — 20.25%**

* These periods should be investigated further to identify potential **pricing, discounting, demand, product-mix, or operating-cost factors** affecting profitability.
